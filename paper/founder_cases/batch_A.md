# Founder firing cases, batch A (20 cases)

Rules: D-35 in DECISIONS.md. Numbers come from the pack (founder_cases_data.csv): the three-day market-model return, the six-week slide against the market, and buy-and-hold returns from day +2 against the matched firm (industry, size, return on assets) and the S&P 500; dollars are millions of 2023 dollars (D-34). Facts come only from the sources named; 'not found' means the sources read are silent. Database notes are the coders' notes in the CEO dismissal database (Gentry et al.), quoted as written.

## 49. Worldcom Inc: Bernard John Ebbers (2002-04-30)

- **What happened:** The board demanded the founder's resignation on 26 April 2002; he resigned as president, CEO and director on 30 April. His personal loans from the company were consolidated into one note of about $408.2 million.
- **Why (sources):** Loans from the company to the CEO: "On April 26, Worldcom's board voted unanimously to demand Ebbers' resignation" (database notes, quoting the filing). Sources: Database notes; WorldCom filing <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723527/000091205702020812/0000912057-02-020812.txt>
- **How:** pushed resignation (board vote demanding it). **Push or planned:** pushed: "Worldcom's board voted unanimously to demand Ebbers' resignation"
- **Successor:** not found in sources read (not found).
- **Shareholders:** bankrupt. Three-day return -34.0%; slide before -51.6%; three years against the matched firm -70% (-$27.4 billion), against the S&P 500 -112% (-$43.7 billion).
- **Competitor:** COX COMMUNICATIONS INC; plausible: y. Cox Communications, a US cable and telecoms operator; same broad business.
- **Founder afterwards:** board: left the board at once (resigned as director on 30 April 2002) (Database notes quoting the filing). Came back as CEO: no. Next: none (in prison from September 2006) (<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Ebbers>). Legal: Indicted on federal securities-fraud and conspiracy charges on 2 March 2004; convicted on all counts on 15 March 2005; sentenced to 25 years. - **News check:** Filing dates the board's demand to 26 April and the resignation to 30 April = day 0; first press report not read. Agree within 3 days: not checked.
